# Frescopa

> Frescopa designs products, services and places around the way people live: subscription plans, care and concierge services, 120 Frescopa Houses in 38 markets, and Frescopa Circle, a free membership with member prices and Velo points.

Plans start at $19 a month with Frescopa Go, which you can pause or switch any month. Frescopa One, from $49 a month, adds Care+, priority Concierge, 3× Velo points and a 60-day guarantee. Household and business plans cost more. Frescopa Circle has more than 4 million members across three tiers: Circle, Circle Plus and Circle Signature. Chat support is available 24/7, and phone support every day from 7am to 11pm ET.
